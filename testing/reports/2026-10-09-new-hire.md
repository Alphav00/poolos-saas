## Findings

### 1. Water test scale abbreviations (TA, CYA, CH) lack plain explanations for new hires

- Feature area: Tech Field Portal
- Severity: Minor
- Device: phone (390×844)
- Persona and journey: new hire, journey 2, step 2
- What happened: The readings entry grid and modal picker display chemical acronyms ("TA", "CYA", "CH", "FC") without full parameter names or plain-language guidance (e.g., "Total Alkalinity", "Cyanuric Acid / Stabilizer", "Calcium Hardness").
- What you expected: A first-week seasonal hire should see plain explanations or full terms explaining what each abbreviation means so they know which drop test reagents to use.
- Steps:
  1. Route tab → tap a pool (e.g. Oak Hollow).
  2. Tap "Enter today's readings".
  3. Observe parameter labels TA, CYA, CH on buttons and modal header.

### 2. Modal test entry sheet lacks drop kit instructions

- Feature area: Tech Field Portal
- Severity: Minor
- Device: phone (390×844)
- Persona and journey: new hire, journey 2, step 2
- What happened: When opening a test entry modal sheet (e.g., pH or Free Chlorine), the modal displays "tap the number" alongside the value wheel, but provides no instructions on how to perform the test with a drop kit in hand.
- What you expected: Each step should state what to do with the drop kit in hand (e.g. sample tube fill level, number of reagent drops or color comparison instructions).
- Steps:
  1. Open a pool → tap "Enter today's readings".
  2. Tap any reading card (e.g., pH or FC).
  3. Inspect the modal sheet content.

### 3. No "Ask a senior" action available on field screens

- Feature area: Tech Field Portal
- Severity: Idea
- Device: phone (390×844)
- Persona and journey: new hire, journey 4, step 1
- What happened: There is no direct "Ask a senior" button on Today, PoolView, or ReadingsEntry screens. The only communication mechanism is navigating to the Message board ("💬 Message board" on Today or the Messages tab).
- What you expected: An "Ask a senior" button reachable from any screen so a seasonal hire can quickly get assistance from a lead tech or dispatcher when unsure.
- Steps:
  1. Open Today, PoolView, or ReadingsEntry screen.
  2. Look for a quick action to contact a senior technician or lead.
